Today my journey to becoming a game programmer officially starts.

I can write variables, conditions and loops, and not much more. The goal, on the other hand, is big: to work at Rockstar Games one day. I know it won't happen tomorrow, and that's fine.

## The plan

- **Phase 1, until March 2027:** programming fundamentals with CS50x, and C++ with learncpp.
- **Phase 2, in parallel:** the math games need, from vectors to matrices.
- **Phase 3:** my first 2D games in C++ with raylib.
- **Phase 4:** 3D, a small OpenGL engine and Unreal Engine.
- **Phase 5:** portfolio projects and first job applications, by the end of 2028.

## Why a devlog

Two reasons. Writing down what I learn forces me to really understand it. And in two years, this site will be the record of the journey: every game, every mistake, every step forward.

## This week

Setting up my tools, creating my GitHub account and starting CS50x Lecture 0.
